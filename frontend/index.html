How to get your data
01 Go to takeout.google.com → deselect all → check My Activity
02 Click "Multiple formats" → set My Activity format to JSON
03 Download & extract → find Takeout/My Activity/Search/MyActivity.json
04 Drop it above. Data is sent only to localhost:8080 — never leaves your machine.

Words only you search.
The C++ engine ran — unique queries through a TF-IDF model — weighting terms by how frequent they are in your history versus how common they are in general English. What surfaces are the words statistically unique to your search fingerprint.
tf = term frequency across your weighted query corpus
idf = log(N / df) blended with general-English prior
score = tf × idf — higher means more you
